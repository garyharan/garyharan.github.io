My management style

Trust first, verify by exception

I won't micromanage. I assume you're capable and give you room to work. I check in when something seems off. I love code though and sometimes might make suggestions.

Growth is the job

I think about your career development as much as the roadmap. Tell me where you want to go. I will help you get there. Oftentimes I'll be working to improve the processes that help or hinder the team.

I'll be your advocate

Visibility matters. I'll toot your horn in the rooms you're not in and make sure your work gets the credit it deserves.

I always assume good intent first

When something looks off, I start from the assumption that you had good reasons. We figure out what happened together before jumping to conclusions.

What I ask of others

Transparency over perfection

I want the right people to know when something goes wrong. Not after we've fixed it. I love getting bad news early.

Own your domain

I delegate with intent. When something is yours, it's yours. If you need help with something, I'll be there.

Pride in your craft

Standards matter. I care about code quality, design decisions, and doing things right. I have strong opinions about cyclomatic complexity and naming things. I will never complain if you include me in naming a project, model or function.

A few honest limitations

DevOps is not my domain. I won't pretend otherwise. I'll find you the right resource, not fake expertise I don't have.
I can't read your mind. If you need a break, tell me. I'll nearly always say yes. I might not notice if you don't say anything.
I won't be as involved in the code as you. Management requires time handling non-technical stuff. I'll dive deep and pair with you when you ask for it and time allows, but I'll defer to your expertise on code-related issues.
